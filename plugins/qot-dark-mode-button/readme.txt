A small button on your posts that switches the post to light text on a dark background, and back. Only the post changes.

== Description ==

Some readers find light text on a dark background easier on the eyes, especially at night. QOT Dark Mode Button gives them a switch right on your posts. One click turns the post dark; another click brings it back.

Only the post changes. Your header, menus, sidebar and footer stay exactly as you designed them, so there is nothing to test across your whole site and nothing that can clash with your theme.

**What you get**

* **One button**, at the top of the post text or floating in a corner.
* **The whole post box or just its text** turns dark, your choice.
* **Your own dark colours** for background, text and links. The defaults are soft greys and a gentle blue, easier on the eyes than pure black and white.
* **Your own button:** moon and sun icons with text, text only or icon only, your own words and colours.
* **Remembers each reader's choice** in their own browser, so someone who likes dark posts gets them on every post.
* **Can follow the reader's device:** optionally start dark when their phone or computer is in dark mode, until they choose otherwise.
* **A live preview** on the settings page. Click its button to try it.

**Light by design**

* About 2 KB of inline CSS and script, only on posts that show the button.
* No page jump: the dark background is drawn around the post without changing its size.
* No cookies, no tracking, no calls to other sites.

**Good to know:** the button appears on your posts as soon as you activate the plugin. Visit Settings > QOT Dark Mode Button to change it.

== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress admin, go to Plugins > Add New Plugin and search for "QOT Dark Mode Button".
2. Click Install Now, then Activate.
3. Open any post and try the button. Change it in Settings > QOT Dark Mode Button.

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does it change my whole site to dark mode? =

No, on purpose. Only the post (or only its text) changes, so your design stays safe.

= Some coloured blocks in my post keep their colours. Why? =

Blocks where you chose your own text or background colour keep them, so they still look the way you made them.

= Does it work with my theme? =

It works with classic and block themes. It wraps the post text so it can always find it, then darkens the post box around it.

= Can I hide the button on one post? =

Developers can use the `qot_dark_mode_button_show` filter and return false for that post.

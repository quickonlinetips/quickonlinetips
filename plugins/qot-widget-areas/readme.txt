Adds new widget areas to any theme: below the header, before and after posts, below the comments, around the sidebar and above the footer.

== Description ==

Many themes have just one sidebar, so there's no place for a newsletter box after your posts or a notice under the header. QOT Widget Areas adds those places to any theme. Activate it, then open Appearance > Widgets: the new areas are there, ready for any widget or block.

**The new areas**

* **Below the header:** a notice bar or banner on every page.
* **Before the post:** above the text of each post.
* **After the post:** a newsletter form, author box or related links.
* **Below the comments:** at the end of the post page.
* **Top and bottom of the sidebar:** around your theme's own sidebar widgets (themes with a sidebar).
* **Above the footer:** a wide area before the footer on every page.

**What you get**

* **Works with classic and block themes.** Block themes even get the Widgets screen back for these areas.
* **Empty areas add nothing,** not even an empty box, so you can switch them all on and fill only the ones you need.
* **Pick your areas:** untick the ones you don't want and they leave the Widgets screen. Their widgets are kept.
* **Posts, pages or both** for the post areas.
* **A map** on the settings page shows where each area goes, and fades any spot your theme doesn't have.

**Light by design**

* No scripts on your site. A few lines of spacing CSS, only on pages that show one of the areas.

Find the settings in Appearance > Widget Areas.

== Installation ==

1. In your WordPress admin, go to Plugins > Add New Plugin and search for "QOT Widget Areas".
2. Click Install Now, then Activate.
3. Open Appearance > Widgets and add widgets to the areas starting with "QOT:".

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= An area says "not in your theme". =

Block themes have no widget sidebar, so the sidebar areas only work in classic themes. Every other area works with both.

= Where exactly does "Below the header" show in a classic theme? =

Classic themes have no standard spot just under the header, so it shows at the very top of the page, using the place WordPress gives every theme. In block themes it sits right below the header.

= The areas before and after the post don't show on my homepage. =

They belong to single posts (and pages if you choose), not to lists of posts.

= What happens to my widgets if I deactivate the plugin? =

WordPress keeps them under Inactive widgets in Appearance > Widgets.

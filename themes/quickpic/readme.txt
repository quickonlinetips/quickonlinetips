A fast one-column theme for photo bloggers: one small stylesheet, no JavaScript, nothing to set up. Activate it and start posting.

== Description ==

QuickPic is for photo bloggers, travel and hobby blogs and anyone who wants their pictures and words to have the whole page. There is no sidebar competing for attention: one framed column puts every post front and centre, and loads instantly, so you spend your time taking pictures and writing instead of learning a page builder.

* **Ultra light and fast.** One small stylesheet, no JavaScript, no web fonts, no frameworks.
* **Plug and play.** Activate it and start posting. There is no options panel to learn.
* **Familiar and simple.** Set up widgets and menus in the usual Appearance > Widgets and Menus screens. Posts written in the block editor look just right.
* **Simple layout.** A green header with your site title, a menu bar, your posts in one column inside a dark frame, and a green box at the bottom with a search form and up to three widget columns. On phones the frame steps aside and the page fills the screen.
* **Room for big photos.** A Wide template gives a post or page about 1060px for large photos and galleries.
* **Custom-coded.** Every file is hand-written and easy to read if you want to change something in a child theme.
* **Modern touches.** Automatic dark mode, text that scales smoothly from phone to desktop, an editor that looks like your site, and clean printouts.

Features: header colour and accent colour pickers, small thumbnails or large photos in post lists, dark mode (can be switched off), Wide template for posts and pages, widget areas in the bottom box (three columns) and below posts, search box in the bottom box, main menu bar and footer menu with social icons, custom logo, custom background, featured images, Updated date on edited posts, sticky posts, threaded comments, numbered pagination, theme colours and text sizes in the block editor, block styles and patterns, starter content for new sites, Skip to content link, print styles, translation ready.

To change the green header or the green links and buttons, go to Appearance > Customize > Colours and pick your own. The same screen lets you turn off dark mode if you want the site to always stay light.

More about the theme: https://www.quickonlinetips.com/archives/quickpic-wordpress-theme/

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does QuickPic support the block editor? =

Yes for writing posts and pages: block content is styled to match the theme. QuickPic itself is a classic theme, so the site layout is set with widgets and menus, not with the Site Editor.

= Where do I add my menu? =

Go to Appearance > Menus, create a menu and tick Main Menu (the bar under the header) or Footer Menu.

= Why does a new site already show widgets and a menu? =

On a brand-new WordPress install, QuickPic offers starter content in Appearance > Customize: three bottom box widgets (About This Site, Recent Posts, Pages), About and Contact pages and a main menu, so you see the whole design straight away. Nothing is saved until you click Publish, and sites that already have content never see it. Edit or remove any of it in Appearance > Widgets.

= Do I need the Classic Widgets plugin? =

No. QuickPic uses the block widgets WordPress gives every site. Add any block (Latest Posts, Categories, Paragraph, Image and more) in Appearance > Widgets.

= What shows in the green box at the bottom before I add widgets? =

The search box, then Recent Posts, Categories and Recent Comments side by side, so a new site never looks empty. As soon as you add your own widgets to Footer 1, 2 or 3, they take over. If your old widgets were moved to Inactive when you switched themes, drag them into the Footer areas in Appearance > Widgets.

= Where can I add widgets? =

Appearance > Widgets has four areas: Footer 1, 2 and 3 (columns in the green box at the bottom of every page) and Below Posts (under each single post, before the comments). Empty areas are hidden, so add only what you need.

= How do I give a post more room for big photos? =

In the editor, open the Post (or Page) settings, find Template and pick "Wide, for big photos". That post gets a page about 1180px wide instead of 780px.

= Can I show full posts on the home page, or shorter excerpts? =

Yes. Appearance > Customize > Post Lists lets you show a short excerpt with Read More (the default) or the full post, and set the excerpt length in words. With full posts, add a More block in a post to choose where it breaks. Search results always show excerpts.

= Can the home page show bigger photos? =

Yes. In Appearance > Customize > Post Lists, set Featured photos to "A large photo across the column". Each post's featured image then shows across the whole column above its excerpt, so the home page reads like a gallery of stories. The default is the classic small thumbnail on the right.

= How do I add social icons? =

Create a menu in Appearance > Menus with links to your profiles (for example https://www.instagram.com/yourname) and assign it to Footer Menu. Links to well-known networks show as small icons; other links stay as text.

= Can I turn off dark mode? =

Yes. Appearance > Customize > Colours, untick "Dark mode for readers who use it".

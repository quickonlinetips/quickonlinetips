A bold, poster-style magazine block theme: huge headlines, a sunny masthead and hard-shadow cards. Fast, with no JavaScript.

== Description ==

QuickPop is for bloggers, creators and small magazines who want a site that looks like a cover story, not like every other blog. Readers remember the big headlines and bright colours, and you get a magazine look without a page builder.

* **Loud and memorable.** Poster headlines, a yellow masthead, cards with hard coloured shadows and bright boxes for tips and notes.
* **Magazine home page.** The newest post opens as a big lead story with its photo, then the rest follow in a poster grid with page numbers.
* **Big single posts.** A poster headline, a framed featured image, an Updated date when you edit a post, tags as chips, comments and three more stories at the end.
* **Fast.** One small stylesheet, one bundled font (Inter Display Black) and no JavaScript of its own.
* **Ready-made patterns.** Poster hero, colour boxes, big idea callout, call to action and an about-the-author card, all in the QuickPop pattern category.
* **Modern touches.** Automatic dark mode, text that scales smoothly from phone to desktop, and an editor that looks like your site.

Features: block theme for the Site Editor, automatic dark mode (can be switched off), lead story and post grid, 12-colour palette, poster font sizes, block styles (Pop card, Framed image, Poster heading, Outline button), six patterns, wide page template, custom logo, menus, featured images, Updated date on edited posts, sticky posts marked with a star, threaded comments, print styles, translation ready.

More about the theme: https://www.quickonlinetips.com/

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= How do I change the menu? =

Go to Appearance > Editor > Navigation and edit the menu, or open the Header in the Site Editor and select the menu. Until you make a menu, QuickPop lists your pages.

= How does the home page choose the lead story? =

The newest post becomes the big lead story with its featured image. The grid below starts from the next post, so nothing appears twice.

= How do I turn off dark mode? =

QuickPop follows the visitor's device setting. To keep the site always light, go to Appearance > Editor > Styles, open Browse styles and choose "Always light".

= How do I change the colours? =

Go to Appearance > Editor > Styles > Colors. The yellow, coral, mint, pink and sky colours can all be changed there and the whole site follows.

= Where are the patterns? =

In the block editor, open the inserter, choose Patterns and pick the QuickPop category: Poster hero, Colour boxes, Big idea, Call to action and About the author.

= How do I make a landing page without the title? =

Edit the page, open the Template panel and choose "Wide page, no title band". Then start the page with the Poster hero pattern.

= Does QuickPop load JavaScript? =

Not of its own. The menu, cards, dark mode and hover effects are all CSS. WordPress itself may add small scripts for features you turn on, such as comment replies.

A fast three-column blog theme with room for widgets: one small stylesheet, no JavaScript, nothing to set up. Activate it and start writing.

== Description ==

QuickPress is for bloggers and growing sites that want room for widgets (recent posts, categories, a newsletter box, an ad or two) without a slow, cluttered page. Your readers get your posts first and fast, and you spend your time writing instead of learning a page builder.

* **Ultra light and fast.** One small stylesheet, no JavaScript, no web fonts, no frameworks.
* **Plug and play.** Activate it and start writing. There is no options panel to learn.
* **Familiar and simple.** Set up widgets and menus in the usual Appearance > Widgets and Menus screens. Posts written in the block editor look just right.
* **Room for widgets.** Posts on the left; on the right a full-width widget area with two narrow columns below it, so the sidebar can be one, two or three columns. On phones the posts come first, then the widgets.
* **Custom-coded.** Every file is hand-written and easy to read if you want to change something in a child theme.
* **Modern touches.** Automatic dark mode, text that scales smoothly from phone to desktop, an editor that looks like your site, and clean printouts.

Features: header colour and accent colour pickers, dark mode (can be switched off), Full width template for posts and pages, widget areas in the sidebar (full width plus two split columns), below posts and in three footer columns, search box in the header, main menu bar and footer menu with social icons, custom logo, custom background, featured images, Updated date on edited posts, sticky posts, threaded comments, numbered pagination, theme colours and text sizes in the block editor, block styles and patterns, starter content for new sites, Skip to content link, print styles, translation ready.

To change the blue header or the pink accent, go to Appearance > Customize > Colours and pick your own. The same screen lets you turn off dark mode if you want the site to always stay light.

More about the theme: https://www.quickonlinetips.com/archives/quickpress-wordpress-theme/

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does QuickPress support the block editor? =

Yes for writing posts and pages: block content is styled to match the theme. QuickPress itself is a classic theme, so the site layout is set with widgets and menus, not with the Site Editor.

= Where do I add my menu? =

Go to Appearance > Menus, create a menu and tick Main Menu (the bar under the header) or Footer Menu.

= Why does a new site already show widgets and a menu? =

On a brand-new WordPress install, QuickPress offers starter content in Appearance > Customize: sidebar widgets (Recent Posts, Categories, Recent Comments), two footer columns (About, Pages), About and Contact pages and a main menu, so you see the whole design straight away. Nothing is saved until you click Publish, and sites that already have content never see it. Edit or remove any of it in Appearance > Widgets.

= Do I need the Classic Widgets plugin? =

No. QuickPress uses the block widgets WordPress gives every site. Add any block (Latest Posts, Categories, Paragraph, Image and more) in Appearance > Widgets.

= What shows in the sidebar before I add widgets? =

Recent Posts across the top, then Categories and Recent Comments side by side, so a new site never looks empty. As soon as you add your own widgets, they take over. If your old widgets were moved to Inactive when you switched themes, drag them into the sidebar areas in Appearance > Widgets.

= How do the sidebar areas work? =

Sidebar Full runs across the whole right column. Sidebar Split Left and Sidebar Split Right sit side by side under it. Use only Sidebar Full for a two-column site, or all three for three columns. A split area on its own takes the full width.

= How do I make a page without the sidebar? =

In the editor, open the Post (or Page) settings, find Template and pick "Full width, no sidebar".

= Where can I add widgets? =

Appearance > Widgets has seven areas: Sidebar Full, Sidebar Split Left, Sidebar Split Right, Below Posts (under each single post, before the comments) and Footer 1, 2 and 3. Empty areas are hidden, so add only what you need.

= Can I show full posts on the home page, or shorter excerpts? =

Yes. Appearance > Customize > Post Lists lets you show a short excerpt with Read More (the default) or the full post, and set the excerpt length in words. With full posts, add a More block in a post to choose where it breaks. Search results always show excerpts.

= How do I add social icons? =

Create a menu in Appearance > Menus with links to your profiles (for example https://www.instagram.com/yourname) and assign it to Footer Menu. Links to well-known networks show as small icons; other links stay as text.

= Can I turn off dark mode? =

Yes. Appearance > Customize > Colours, untick "Dark mode for readers who use it".
